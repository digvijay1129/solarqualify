# Phase 4.11 — Quotation Management and PDF Generation

## Purpose

This phase adds the formal quotation lifecycle on top of the deterministic Phase 4.10 calculation engine. It does not use AI to calculate prices and does not recalculate financial values when a quotation is viewed or downloaded.

A formal quotation can only be generated by assigned Sales Staff for a lead whose workflow status is `qualified` and which has a persisted lead score and scoring version. The calculation engine supplies financial values and active, effective-dated pricing, generation and verified subsidy rule snapshots. The generated quotation stores a customer/property snapshot and is promoted from `draft` to `generated`.

## APIs

| Method | Endpoint | Access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/api/v1/leads/:leadId/quotations` | Assigned Sales Staff | Generate a formal quotation |
| GET | `/api/v1/quotations/:quotationId` | Lead's homeowner or assigned Sales Staff | Retrieve quotation details |
| GET | `/api/v1/quotations/:quotationId/pdf` | Lead's homeowner or assigned Sales Staff | Download quotation PDF |
| GET | `/api/v1/leads/:leadId/quotations` | Lead's homeowner or assigned Sales Staff | Retrieve quotation history |

The creation payload accepts Phase 4.10's optional `effectiveOn`, `pricingRuleId`, `subsidyRuleId` and `generationRuleId` selectors.

## Financial breakdown

- Equipment cost = system capacity × price per kW.
- Installation charges = fixed installation amount from the selected pricing rule.
- Gross system cost = equipment cost + installation charges.
- Applicable subsidy = configured verified marginal subsidy slabs, limited by the policy maximum and gross cost.
- Net installation cost = gross system cost − applicable subsidy.
- Estimated savings and payback retain the Phase 4.10 formula and input limitations.

Equipment and installation amounts are stored separately alongside gross and net totals.

## PDF generation

PDFs are generated server-side from saved quotation records and their snapshots. The PDF includes quotation number and status, customer and property details, system capacity and generation, consumption coverage, equipment and installation costs, gross cost, configured subsidy, net cost, estimated savings/payback, rule versions and calculation explanations.

PDFs are rendered on demand; viewing or downloading never recalculates a quotation against current rules. Downloads use attachment and private no-cache headers. Draft calculation records cannot be downloaded as formal quotations. The built-in PDF writer adds no runtime dependency or public storage URL.

## Access and history

Sales Staff may create quotations only for leads assigned to them. Homeowners and Sales Staff may retrieve details and history only when they can access the parent lead. A quotation ID alone does not grant access.

History includes all saved quotation records for the lead, including earlier draft calculation records. Formal PDF downloads are available only after generation.

## Limitations

The current lead/rule models do not contain panel make/model, inverter model, panel count, roof layout, structural survey or detailed electrical design. The quotation displays configured system capacity and financial estimates; it does not invent equipment brands, quantities or technical specifications. Final sizing and installation scope require a site survey.

Configured subsidy estimates do not establish individual government eligibility or guarantee approval. Generation, savings and payback are estimates, not guarantees.
